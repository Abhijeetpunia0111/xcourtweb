About the founder

A home in Hyderabad
for every sport.

Crosscourt Sports Club began with a simple idea: one address in Hyderabad where anyone can walk in, pick a sport and play, with courts, coaching and a community that match the ambition of the people using them.

Today the club runs eight sports across ITF-standard courts, a pool, a gym and a shaded lounge, open 365 days a year in Gandipet.
